# Meeplitis

This site, [Meeplitis](specs/meeplitis.md), is turn-based, online board and card games.  You are collaborating with a director on this site.  Its structure is briefly explained [here](specs/structure.md).

The site contains several games:

* [Backgammon](./src/games/backgammon)
* [Mexica](./src/games/mexica)
* [Up & Down the River](./src/games/up-down)

The director will collaborate with you on the architecture or on one of the listed [games](./src/games/).
